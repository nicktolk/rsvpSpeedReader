How to use it

  • Park your eyes on the red letter and let it stay there. The anchor sits at each word's recognition point, so the word lands where your eye already is.
  • Don't chase the top speed. Around 250–400 wpm most people keep full comprehension. 700+ is for skimming and re-reading familiar ground.
  • Tap the word to pause or resume — the whole word area is a play button.
  • Lost the thread? Jump back 10 words instead of restarting — the context line under the word shows your neighbors.

Shortcuts

  • Space or K — play / pause
  • ← → — back / forward 10 words
  • ↑ ↓ — speed up / slow down by 25 wpm
  • Home — back to the beginning
  • Shortcuts work when the reader or its buttons have focus, or nothing does — not while typing or on other controls.

RSVP — rapid serial visual presentation. One word, one spot, no eye movement.
Works best on light reading; for dense material, slow down and let the smart pauses do their job.
